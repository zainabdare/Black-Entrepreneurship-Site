THE SETLIST WHY

Why We Built SETLIST Marketplace

SETLIST is a campus-first marketplace connecting LSU students directly with local Black-owned businesses. Whether you need a fresh set, event photos, or game-day catering, we replace fragmented word-of-mouth with verified pricing, direct booking links, and distance filtering—all in one place.
