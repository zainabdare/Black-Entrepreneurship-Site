Empowering Black Business Around lsu Campus

Bridging the gap between Louisiana State University students, faculty, and local residents with talented Black entrepreneurs across Baton Rouge.

Why We Built black-owned BR

As students and creatives living in Baton Rouge, we noticed that finding reliable, high-quality Black service providers—whether looking for silk presses, precision lash fills, game day tailgating plates, or graduation portraits—often relied solely on fragmented word-of-mouth or buried Instagram hashtags.

At the same time, hundreds of hardworking Black entrepreneurs, student hustlers, and alumni small business owners operate within miles of LSU without a unified directory to showcase their craft, pricing, booking links, and service radius.

Black-Owned BR was created to solve this problem: a modern, mobile-friendly directory that connects the LSU community directly to local Black-owned businesses within a 25-mile radius of LSU’s historic Memorial Tower.
